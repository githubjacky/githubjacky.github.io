The note builds CART from the empirical risk it minimizes, scoring each split by the drop in node impurity (sample variance for regression, Gini impurity for classification), then covers when a fully grown tree stops, the early-stopping thresholds, and pruning by a per-leaf penalty chosen through cross-validation.
