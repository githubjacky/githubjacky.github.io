The note builds DBSCAN from $$\varepsilon$$-neighborhoods and core points, grows each cluster outward from a core point, contrasts it with K-means, whose cluster count is an input rather than a result, and picks $$\varepsilon$$ from the elbow of the sorted $$m$$-th smallest distances, checked against noise and largest-cluster shares.
